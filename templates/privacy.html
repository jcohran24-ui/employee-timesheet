JC Timesheet Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

JC Timesheet is an employee timekeeping application used to record work hours, manage employee access, and send work-related account and timesheet notifications.

Information We Collect

We may collect employee names, mobile phone numbers, login/account information, timesheet entries, work-hour totals, and related administrative records.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the timesheet system, authenticate employees, maintain time records, send timesheet emails, and send work-related SMS messages such as account setup instructions, temporary PINs, PIN-reset information, and other non-marketing account notifications.

SMS Messaging

Mobile phone numbers are used only for work-related operational messages associated with JC Timesheet. SMS messages are not used for advertising or promotional marketing. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on account activity.

Employees may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact the administrator who manages the employee's JC Timesheet account.

Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold or rented. Information may be shared only with service providers necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database, email, and SMS providers, or when required by law.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect account and timesheet information. Employee PINs are stored using secure password hashing and are not stored in readable plain text.

Data Retention

Timesheet and account records may be retained as needed for business, payroll, recordkeeping, legal, or administrative purposes. Deleting or deactivating an employee login does not necessarily delete historical timesheet records.

Your Choices

Employees may request correction of account information or removal from SMS notifications by contacting their employer or JC Timesheet administrator.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the employer or administrator responsible for the JC Timesheet account.
